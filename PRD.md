## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The proposed digital product is an ultra-premium, editorial-grade photography studio web platform specifically crafted for the luxury Indian celebration and portraiture market.

Unlike standard mass-market photography portals or generic template portfolios, this platform positions the studio as a high-end visual storytelling house. It marries the emotional depth of traditional and contemporary Indian rituals with the restrained aesthetic sophistication of high-fashion publications (such as *Vogue India*, *Architectural Digest India*, or luxury bridal ateliers). 

The platform serves as an immersive, narrative-driven sales vehicle. Every design element—from whitespace pacing and subtle depth physics to bespoke color harmony—is engineered to guide affluent couples, families, and creative directors through an emotional journey that concludes in high-intent, low-friction business inquiries.

---

## 2. PRODUCT VISION
### 2.1 The Core Thesis
> *"We don't simply take photographs. We preserve the feeling of the moment."*

The website must immediately project:
**LUXURY + TRUST + EMOTION + PHOTOGRAPHY QUALITY + MODERN INDIAN AESTHETIC + HIGH-TOUCH SERVICE**

### 2.2 Design Philosophy: Contemporary Indian Luxury
The visual identity represents a deliberate paradigm shift away from both dark, moody cinematic tropes and kitschy, stereotyped ethnic motifs. 

* **The Departure from the Previous Concept:** The previous dark, black-background cinematic photography concept is explicitly retired. The new platform embraces warmth, luminosity, breathing room, and daylight-infused luxury.
* **Modern Indian Nuance:** The cultural identity emerges organically through authentic people, intimate rituals, architectural heritage, and editorial composition—never through visual clichés such as paisley stamps, cartoon turbans, excessive crimson/maroon palettes, heavy yellow golds, or temple tracery borders.
* **Sensory Cues:** Inspired by handwoven sherwani silks, raw ivory linens, soft peach organzas, champagne bridal embroideries, sandstone heritage courtyards (Jaipur, Jodhpur), and the restrained elegance of modern Indian luxury couture.

## 3. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE & SALES FUNNEL
### 3.1 Primary Business Goal
**To convert discerning visitors into direct, high-value photography inquiries with minimal friction.**

The website is not an archival image repository; it is a high-conversion sales engine designed to qualify leads and establish luxury credibility within 5 seconds of landing.

### 3.2 Target Funnel Architecture
```
[ DISCOVER ]  — Full-viewport visual hero creates instant emotional immersion (0–3s)
     ↓
  [ FEEL ]    — Narrative storytelling & editorial couples' stories evoke aspiration (3–15s)
     ↓
 [ TRUST ]    — Studio philosophy, transparent methodology, & authentic social proof (15–45s)
     ↓
[ EXPLORE ]   — Experiential service breakdown & curated story archives (45–90s)
     ↓
 [ ENQUIRE ]  — Strategic WhatsApp conversion trigger + low-friction custom enquiry
```

### 3.3 Conversion Hierarchy
1. **Primary Conversion (Tier 1):** Direct **WhatsApp Chat Initiation** with pre-populated contextual intent.
2. **Secondary Conversion (Tier 2):** Tailored **Enquiry Form Submission** for scheduled consultations.
3. **Tertiary Conversion (Tier 3):** Full portfolio story exploration and engagement with specific gallery collections.

## 18. PHOTOGRAPHY ART DIRECTION & CURATION
All curated photography assets must adhere to strict visual criteria:
* **Authentic Emotion:** Laughter caught mid-breath, tearful hugs during Vidaai, candid banter during Baraat, intimate whispers between couples.
* **Natural Human Tone:** Rich, authentic Indian skin tones with warmth and natural texture (avoiding over-smoothed or artificially bleached skin).
* **Color Palette Alignment:** Warm ambient light, natural sunlight, glowing diyas, candlelit mandaps, turmeric yellow hues of Haldi, organic henna greens.
* **Composition:** Editorial symmetry, negative space, framing through Rajasthani jharokhas or modern architectural arches, motion blur for joyous dance (Sangeet).
* **Ban on Generic Stock:** No stiff handshake poses, artificial corporate lighting, or foreign stock models wearing inaccurate regional wedding costumes.

## 20. VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM & COLOR PALETTE
The palette is rooted in understated Indian high fashion and heritage architecture:

### 20.1 Core Color Tokens
* **Base Warm White (`#FAF8F5`):** Clean, breathable, soft daylight canvas that rejects sterile clinical `#FFFFFF`.
* **Ivory Canvas (`#F4EFEB`):** Soft, tactile paper tone for section alternations and card surfaces.
* **Sherwani Silk Beige (`#EADCC9`):** Grounded architectural tone inspired by unbleached silk and Jodhpur sandstone.
* **Soft Peach Organza (`#F7E7DC`):** Delicate, warm romantic blush for interactive hovers and badge highlights.
* **Champagne Accent (`#DFD3C3`):** Refined border, divider, and subtle embellishment tone.
* **Subtle Warm Gold (`#B89762`):** Muted metallic gold used sparingly for micro-badges, accents, and progress indicators (Strictly NO loud glitter/yellow gold).
* **Deep Charcoal Espresso (`#2C2523`):** High-contrast, warm dark brown for typography that feels softer and more luxurious than pitch `#000000`.
* **Secondary Warm Charcoal (`#5A4E4B`):** Muted body copy and metadata styling.

### 20.2 Typography System
* **Display / Editorial Serif:**  
  * *Primary Candidates:* `Cormorant Garamond`, `Playfair Display`, or `Cinzel` (Editorial, romantic, high-fashion cadence).
  * *Usage:* Headlines (H1, H2), hero title, quotation blocks, story titles.
* **Body / Interface Sans-Serif:**  
  * *Primary Candidates:* `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `Montserrat`.
  * *Usage:* Body text, navigation links, buttons, metadata labels, inputs.
* **Stylistic Hierarchy:** Generous letter-spacing (tracking) on uppercase labels, high line-height on editorial text for relaxed reading rhythm.

## 21. 3D, MOTION & SPATIAL DEPTH SPECIFICATION
To achieve a subtle, dimensional luxury feeling without turning into an overwhelming WebGL tech demo:

* **Layered Parallax:** Subtle multi-plane scrolling where foreground editorial text floats smoothly over gently moving photography planes.
* **Ken Burns Hero Animation:** Very slow (20–30s scale factor 1.00 -> 1.04) continuous pan and zoom, providing cinematic breath to hero slides.
* **Atmospheric Shadows:** Extremely soft, wide-radius ambient shadows (`box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(44, 37, 35, 0.08)`).
* **Pointer Perspective (Desktop Only):** Micro-tilt (1–2 degrees maximum) on hovered editorial story cards, creating physical depth like handling fine art cardstock.
* **Strict Anti-Patterns:**
  * NO spinning 3D cubes, polygons, or glTF models.
  * NO particle meshes or artificial glittering dust.
  * NO aggressive spring physics or jarring page transitions.

## 24. ACCESSIBILITY (a11y) STANDARDS
* **Compliance Target:** WCAG 2.1 Level AA.
* **Contrast Ratios:** Minimum 4.5:1 ratio between text and ivory/peach backgrounds. Body typography must use Deep Charcoal Espresso (`#2C2523`) rather than low-contrast gray.
* **Keyboard Navigation:** Complete focus-visible rings with logical tab sequences across nav items, hero carousels, and form elements.
* **Screen Reader Readiness:** Descriptive ARIA attributes on interactive icon buttons (e.g., carousel arrows, mobile menu toggles, WhatsApp triggers).
* **Motion Preferences:** Full support for `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, instantly falling back to simple opacity fades with zero parallax or Ken Burns scaling.
